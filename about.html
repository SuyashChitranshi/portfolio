Hello! I'm Suyash

I'm a Senior UX Designer with over 6 years of experience, and the part of the job I care most about is figuring out why something isn't working before I touch a screen. Most of my projects start with a friction point — something users are struggling with that nobody's quite named yet — and my first move is always to understand what's actually happening, not jump to a fix.

I have a background in Computer Engineering, which shaped how I think about problems — I like breaking things down, looking for patterns, and understanding the system underneath the surface issue. I work in Power BI and I'm comfortable with data-driven thinking, and alongside my design work I've also built executive-facing reports and proposal materials for leadership — so I'm used to translating findings for people who need the short, clear version, not the whole process.

I'm drawn to messy, ambiguous problems more than clean briefs — the kind where you have to dig into what users are actually doing before you know what to design. I think good design follows good understanding of the problem, and I'm looking to grow further in the data and measurement side of that — using research and evidence to guide decisions, not just intuition.
