01

The Problem: Decision Paralysis & Lack of Clarity

Existing learning platforms were fragmented, content-heavy, and failed to guide employees toward role-specific mastery.

67% of employees struggled to identify the right learning path due to cluttered and unstructured course catalogs

63% of enrolled learners failed to complete role-relevant programs because progress and outcomes were unclear.

warning Core Pain Point

"I never know what I’m supposed to complete next. Courses are spread across different sections, and there’s no clear view of my progress or priorities."
— Anonymous Learner

verified_user Constraints Faced

  • watch

    Time Constraints

    The platform had to be designed and rolled out alongside ongoing learning programs, limiting the ability to pause or restructure existing content flows. UX decisions needed to prioritize incremental clarity improvements over large-scale system changes.

  • architecture

    Technical Constraints

    The LMS was built on a legacy learning infrastructure with fixed role taxonomies, course metadata, and certification logic. This restricted deep personalization and required UX solutions that worked within existing data structures rather than redefining them.

  • psychology

    User Constraints

    Learners had highly varied experience levels—from fresh hires to senior engineers—making it necessary to balance simplicity for new users with depth for advanced learners, without fragmenting the experience.

  • business

    Organizational Constraints

    Design decisions needed to align with L&D governance models, approval workflows, and compliance requirements already in place. Stakeholder alignment across HR, engineering, and leadership influenced prioritization and scope throughout the project.

02

Expected Outcomes

The redesigned learning experience was expected to improve clarity around what to learn, why it matters, and how progress is measured, enabling employees to engage with role-relevant learning paths confidently and with reduced reliance on external guidance.

By structuring learning journeys and making progression visible, the platform aimed to shift learning behavior from passive exploration to goal-oriented skill development, while giving L&D and leadership teams better visibility into learning outcomes.

Expected Outcomes Description
Reduce learning discovery time This directly addressed the issue where 67% of employees struggled to identify the right learning path due to cluttered and unstructured course catalogs.
Increase learning completion rates By introducing clear progression stages and certifications, the design aimed to reduce drop-offs, addressing the problem where 63% of enrolled learners failed to complete role-relevant programs
Improve visibility into skill readiness This outcome targeted the gap where 69% of L&D stakeholders lacked visibility into employee skill maturity and role readiness across teams.
Standardize learning across roles The role-based framework was designed to reduce redundant learning and inconsistency by providing structured, repeatable learning paths aligned to organizational roles.
03

Key Design Decisions

Decision #1

Implementation of Outcome-Driven Progress Frameworks

Improving the efficiency of user progression was achieved by replacing open-ended exploration with structured milestones:

01. Maturity Models

Designers introduced a 5-stage learning maturity model (Aware, Equipped, Competent, Expert, and Certification) to provide a clear roadmap for skill mastery.

02. Progress Transparency

The use of 0% → 100% progress indicators, modular progress cards, and at-a-glance metrics (such as total learning time) helped reduce the cognitive effort required to track one’s own status.

Decision #2

Decision Reduction through Multi-Layered Personalization

To combat "decision paralysis" and content fatigue in large-scale environments, the Talent Academy implemented a three-layer recommendation framework. This system automatically maps learning paths to an employee's organization-mapped role, primary skill area, and functional role, allowing users to find actionable content within seconds.
